# Companion — roadmap

What is planned next. Shipped features are described in the [README](README.md).

## The room

The room is Companion's new design: a page-style note editor, a calendar that knows your
timetable, an inbox for quick thoughts, search, and Sage. Still to come:

- **Word count** for a note.
- **Due dates and reminders** on notes.
- **Sage on long notes** — run an oversized note section by section on its own (today you pick
  the section).

### Later
- **Sage quizzes** — questions generated from a note, with answers checked.
- **Lofi radio** — a small player on Home, off by default.

## Platform

- **Read-only share links** for a note.
- **Continuous integration** — build, lint and unit tests on every push.
- **Error monitoring.**
- **Tests for the save layer** — the per-block delta save and the legacy-schema migration.

### Later
- **Installable app** (PWA) that opens offline.

## Launch (later)

- **A public landing page**, with screenshots and a social card.
- **Self-hosted fonts.**
- **A real logo** in place of the placeholder crest.
